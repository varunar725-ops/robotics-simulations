## What Is Inside This Website?
An interactive 3D WebGL robotics simulation portal and 34-year engineering encyclopedia (1992 – 2026) built with **Three.js**:

- **Main Portal & Live 3D Stage (`index.html`)** — Introduction, interactive multi-robot preview, archival field photography, 6 core engineering pillars, and a filterable **1992–2026 Innovations Timeline**.
- **7 Dedicated Robot 3D Sub-Webpages (`robots/*.html`)**:
  1. **Atlas** — 2026 Production 56-DOF All-Electric Atlas & 2016–2024 Hydraulic HD Atlas (*360° floor get-up & swivel, standing backflip, 180° waist-swivel part transfer, parkour vault, sprint*).
  2. **Spot & Spot Arm** — Spot 5.2 + 6-DOF Arm & Spot Cam 2 (*"Chicken-head" gyro IK stabilization, rough-terrain trot, valve/door manipulation, stair climb, dance, roll self-righting*).
  3. **Stretch** — Nov 2026 Stretch Gen 2 & Gen 1 (*1,000-case/hr dual multipick unloading, >360° continuous-rotation joints, omnidirectional base, dark-trailer vision scan*).
  4. **Handle** — 2019 Counterbalanced Warehouse Raptor & 2017 Wheeled-Humanoid (*4-ft hurdle jump, swinging-tail crate counterbalance, banked figure-8 carve, 360° squat spin*).
  5. **BigDog & LS3 (AlphaDog)** — *Ice-slip lateral kick cross-step recovery, 340–400 lb rough-terrain march, 7 mph bound, cylindrical roll self-righting*.
  6. **Cheetah & WildCat** — *28.3 mph world-record articulated-spine rotary gallop, elastic bounding, 25° banked outdoor turn, emergency skid-stop*.
  7. **SandFlea & RHex** — *30-ft CO₂ ballistic rooftop launch with mid-air wheel-gyro stabilization & 6-legged Buehler-clock compliant C-leg scrambling*.
- **6 Switchable 3D Environments** — Waltham R&D Lab, Automotive Factory, Rocky Mars Trail, Arctic Sheet Ice, Logistics Dock, and Construction Site.
- **Interactive Push-Recovery (`⚡ Impulse Shove`) & Live Telemetry HUD** — Joint XYZ coordinate axes, Center-of-Mass plumb vector, slow-motion (`0.25x`) playback, and wireframe inspection.
